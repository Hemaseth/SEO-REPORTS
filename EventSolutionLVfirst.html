Start Here — The Convergent Finding
● Flagged independently by all five analyses

This site is a San Francisco clone wearing a Las Vegas name

The lowest-scoring lenses share one root cause: the identity layer was copied from the SF site and never re-pointed to Las Vegas. sameAs on all 30 pages lists linkedin.com/company/event-solutions-san-francisco and facebook.com/eventsolutionssf; the visible Yelp link is yelp.com/biz/event-solutions-san-francisco. The business carries three different names (footer “Event Solutions” · schema “Event Solutions Las Vegas | Event Planners & Designers” · LocalBusiness “Las Vegas Event Solutions Productions, Inc.”). The LocalBusiness block has no @id and is severed from the Rank Math @graph. All 16 posts are authored by "admin"; the only named expert, Sheila Dizon-Burns, is credited nowhere. The About page calls the company “based in the San Francisco Bay Area, with operations in Las Vegas.” One body of work — unify the entity, credit a real author, reframe as LV-native — lifts Schema, Local, GEO, E-E-A-T and AI-citation at once. Everything else is sequenced around it.

Critical & High Findings
● Critical · Crawl / Indexation

The XML sitemap is completely broken

StepURLHTTPResult
1/sitemap.xml301x-redirect-by: Rank Math → /sitemap_index.xml
2/sitemap_index.xml404152 KB themed HTML page — incl. for Googlebot
3post/page/author-sitemap.xml404all child sitemaps unreachable
4/?sitemap=1200only reachable index — undocumented, advertises the 404 URLs

robots.txt has no Sitemap: directive, so even the working query-form index is undiscoverable. Root cause: Rank Math's sitemap rewrite rules were never flushed into .htaccess. Net effect: Google and Bing have zero machine-readable URL discovery; all 16 blog posts hang off a single discovery path (/blogs/) — one point of failure for 53% of the site.

Fix — WP → Settings → Permalinks → Save (flush) → verify /sitemap_index.xml = 200 XML → add Sitemap: to robots.txt → submit in GSC. Hand-built fallback saved at _raw/RECOMMENDED-sitemap.xml.
● Critical · AI Search

GPTBot, ClaudeBot & CCBot blocked by ModSecurity

Verified with canonical bot UA strings (re-tested to resolve a phase-agent disagreement — the GEO report's “200 allowed” table is superseded):

Googlebot / Bingbot / PerplexityBot / OAI-SearchBot200 — allowed
GPTBot (ChatGPT browse/train)406 — blocked ✕
ClaudeBot (Anthropic)406 — blocked ✕
CCBot (Common Crawl → many LLMs)406 — blocked ✕

This is a ModSecurity WAF block, not a robots.txt policy — unintentional and uncontrolled. ChatGPT deep-browsing, Claude, and the Common-Crawl corpus cannot read the site.

Fix — allowlist GPTBot, ClaudeBot, CCBot in ModSecurity, then set explicit robots.txt policy so access is a deliberate choice.
● Critical · Trust / Compliance

No privacy policy or terms — on a lead-capture site running reCAPTCHA

/privacy-policy/, /terms/, /terms-of-service/, /terms-and-conditions/ all return 404; zero footer legal links. The site runs a CF7 lead form and a Brevo newsletter signup, and loads reCAPTCHA v3 on every page — Google's reCAPTCHA terms legally require a linked privacy policy, and CCPA covers Nevada residents. A QRG Trustworthiness-floor failure.

Fix — publish privacy policy + terms with data-collection + Nevada-consumer-rights language; link both in the footer.
● Critical · Trust / Authority

Circular self-awarded authority

/top-event-planners-las-vegas/ is a company-authored post that ranks itself #1 (“1. Event Solutions — That's Us!”). /las-vegas-event-planning-services-2/ then cites being “voted into the Top 10 Event Planners in Las Vegas” as if it were external recognition. Quality Raters are trained to detect and penalize manufactured authority.

Fix — remove the self-ranked list or label it transparently as a self-assessment; replace the location-page claim with a verifiable third-party source — or drop it. Verify with owner first.
● High · Schema / Local

Entity fragmentation & a ~12 km geo error

Orphaned @id-less LocalBusiness; bare LocalBusiness instead of EventPlanner; Organization has no sameAs; BlogPosting schema on the homepage and all 8 service pages; &amp; entities inside JSON-LD sitewide. Separately, geo = 36.1612,-115.2976 (Summerlin/Red Rock) sits ~12 km from the stated 655 S Fort Apache Rd — and that address is schema-only, invisible on every page.

Fix — add @id, upgrade to EventPlanner, link into @graph; verify address vs coordinates with the owner; render the address or declare SAB.
● High · Content / Conversion

Thin money pages · a leaky, unmeasured funnel

/graphic-design/ = 62 words (near-doorway stub); /event-marketing/ 201; /about-us/ 173; homepage 279; 7 of 8 service pages below the 800-word floor; the highest-revenue page /meeting-procurement-hotel-selection/ is the thinnest. No inline lead form on any service page (only /contact-us/ has one); tel: is an untracked static link; zero gtag('event') calls anywhere despite GA4 installed; three high-intent posts route to no service/contact page.

Fix — instrument GA4 lead/call events first; add inline forms to the top 5 service pages; expand the stub/thin pages with LV-specific depth; in-body link the dead-end posts to money pages.
