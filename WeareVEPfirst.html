Start Here — The Convergent Finding
● Flagged independently by Schema, Local, On-Page and GEO lenses

The machine layer doesn't know who you are

The site presents as a 17-year-old Bay Area production firm to humans — phone, email, team, portfolio, awards all visible. To machines it presents as a generic WordPress site. Organization.telephone is null. Organization.address is null. No LocalBusiness node anywhere. No Service schema on any service page. One body of structured-data work — populate the existing Yoast graph and add LocalBusiness + Service — moves the Schema, Local, On-Page and AI-search lenses simultaneously. Everything else is sequenced around it.

The Yoast @graph ships WebPage, WebSite, Organization, ImageObject, BreadcrumbList with six valid sameAs profiles including YouTube. It does not ship the two fields that turn a brand into a callable local business. The phone is visible to a person reading the footer; it is invisible to a model summarising "who do I call for AV in San Francisco."

Fix — populate Organization.telephone and Organization.address; insert a LocalBusiness (or EventPlanning subtype) node with @id, priceRange, areaServed, geo, opening hours. 1–2 hours via Yoast Local SEO plugin or a custom snippet.
● High · Trust / Compliance

Privacy & Terms exist — but are not in the footer

Both /privacy-policy/ and /terms-and-conditions/ return 200 and appear in the page sitemap. Neither is linked from the global footer. The site runs four form-bearing pages (Contact, Sales Questionnaire, Virtual Event Inquiry, newsletter signup) collecting personal data. A direct Trustworthiness gap and a CCPA notice-at-collection exposure.

Fix — add Privacy + Terms links to the existing global footer; add "By submitting, you agree to our Privacy Policy" microcopy beneath every form's submit button. 15 minutes.
● High · On-Page · Discovery

Homepage title & H1 don't compete for service intent

A brand tagline as the single H1 is a defensible visual choice that costs the strongest on-page ranking signal. "Your Vision, Amplified." cannot rank for a query a customer types.

Fix — rework H1 to "Bay Area Event Production · Audio Visual, Lighting, Live Streaming" (or similar service-first wording); move the tagline to a stylised eyebrow above. Rewrite <title> to lead with service: Bay Area Event Production & Audio Visual | Verducci.
● High · On-Page

Audio Visual page meta description over-length · slug defect

The /audio-visual-production-2/ meta description ships 282 characters. Google truncates at ~155 on desktop, ~120 on mobile, and the current snippet cuts mid-sentence. The URL slug itself carries a defect marker: the trailing -2 indicates an earlier /audio-visual-production/ was replaced and inherited an automatic WordPress suffix.

Fix — rewrite to 140–155 chars, leading with service + area. Decide canonical URL — see next finding.
● High · Cannibalisation

Two Audio Visual pages competing for the same intent

/audio-visual/ (slug-clean) and /audio-visual-production-2/ both return 200, both appear in the page sitemap, both target audio-visual rental intent. Link equity divides, neither ranks fully, both surface in autocomplete and confuse internal nav.

Fix — decide one canonical owner (/audio-visual/ is the cleaner URL); 301 the other; rebuild internal links and the Yoast canonical. 1 hour including cache flush.
● High · Conversion

Contact Us is 179 words on a lead-capture site

The page that exists to convert is the thinnest service-level page audited: 179 words. No process explanation, no response-time commitment, no team faces, no FAQ, no third-party proof, no map. The schema on this page does not include ContactPage or LocalBusiness. Every other money page routes traffic to a page where the offer is barely articulated.

Fix — rebuild to ~500 words: what happens after submit, typical reply window, named producer who reads inquiries, 3–4 testimonial pulls, an FAQ block (area served, lead time, deposit, what AV is supplied vs rented), embedded office map. Add ContactPage schema.
● Medium-High · GEO

llms.txt exists — but omits every primary money page

The Yoast-generated /llms.txt lists 5 pages, 1 post, 5 portfolios, 5 testimonials, 5 team members. The 5 pages are Hybrid Events, Webcast Production, Digital Media, Virtual Meetings & Conferences, Video Production. Excluded: Audio Visual, Live Streaming, Weddings, Corporate Events, Lighting, DJ Entertainment, Event Photography, Contact Us, Resources. An AI agent reading the file alone will conclude VEP is a virtual-events specialist — a 2020/2021 truth the rest of the site has moved past.

Fix — regenerate llms.txt with the full service list and a "Key Facts" header: founded, area served, services, phone, email, distinguishing claim. Drop thin taxonomies; keep the curated portfolio + team selections.
